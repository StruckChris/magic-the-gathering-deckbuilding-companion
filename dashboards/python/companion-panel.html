The Archmage's Companion

A knowledge pack for learning how to pilot a deck. A context pack for an agent to talk through a turn with you.

You can use the pack to learn Magic yourself, or share the full pack with an agent. As a context starter, it helps jumpstart the agent's understanding by showing how concepts from its training fit together in play. The agent can then apply those concepts to your cards to help you build a deck, learn to play it, or decide what to do on your turn.
