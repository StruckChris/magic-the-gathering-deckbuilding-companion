# Commander Banned Cards Reference

**Scope:** ordinary multiplayer Commander, including cEDH, not Duel Commander, Brawl, Legacy, or Modern. **Checked:** 1 October 2026. This built-in list records 42 individually named cards banned in all deck roles, one companion-only prohibition, and the additional prohibited categories below.

**Authority:** the Commander section of Wizards' [Banned and Restricted list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list). Recheck that section for current legality rather than borrowing another format's list. If lookup is unavailable, identify this as the dated reference.

## Companion Only

- **Lutri, the Spellchaser:** banned as a companion; permitted in the deck or as commander, subject to normal construction rules. It is not on the Game Changers list in this reference.

Biorhythm is no longer banned. These changes took effect on 9 February 2026. [Official announcement](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-banned-and-restricted-february-9-2026).

## Additional Prohibited Categories

The official Commander list also prohibits:

- Cards with the Conspiracy card type.
- Cards referring to playing for ante.
- The cards prohibited in all formats under Wizards' offensive-content policy, linked from the official list.

These category bans apply in addition to the 42 individual names above. Card eligibility is a separate check: custom cards, playtest cards, nonstandard products, and unreleased cards must not be declared legal merely because their names are absent here. Use current format rules and product-specific guidance.

## When an Opponent Shows One

First confirm the exact card and the agreed format or house rules. A remembered historical list or a disclosed rule-zero agreement is not evidence of cheating. Explain the normal restriction neutrally and distinguish any group's exception from official legality. A Game Changer designation is not a substitute for legality.

Do not recommend removal spells as the solution to an unresolved rules or pregame agreement question. See the [opponent reference guide](opponent-card-reference-guide.md) for the distinction between legality and threat assessment.
