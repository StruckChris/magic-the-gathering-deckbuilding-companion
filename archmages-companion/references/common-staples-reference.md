# Common Staples and Goodstuff Reference

**Purpose:** Help a player recognize familiar tools an opponent may use and understand the functions behind them. This is a built-in, curated reference of 120 cards, organized by function, not an inclusion list or a ranking. Prepared 1 October 2026.

"Common" here means recognizable examples of broadly used roles, not a measured claim about today's usage rate. The selection also includes historical tools and contextual alternatives such as Brainstone; not every entry is claimed to be a universal staple. The selection is not exhaustive. A card's inclusion does not establish its price, power bracket, or fit in the player's deck. Explain outside cards when asked; recommend additions only within the supplied pool unless an outside search is explicitly requested.

Use this knowledge to give precise, informed answers, not to name-drop or sound certain without evidence. Keep the scope near 120 useful examples rather than filling a numerical quota with increasingly marginal cards. A player's idea and available cards remain more important than resemblance to this reference.

Use the [opponent reference guide](opponent-card-reference-guide.md) for response boundaries. Consult the separate [Game Changers](commander-game-changers.md) and [banned cards](commander-banned-cards.md) references for official status. The [watch list](opponent-watch-list.md) adds less obvious interactions and enabling pieces; appearing in only one of these curated files is an organizational choice, not a power judgment.

## Mana and Land Development

Distinguish acceleration, fixing, temporary mana, and finding future land drops. Putting a land into hand does not itself increase mana available on the battlefield. Conditional land searches can nevertheless sustain development or replenish a hand.

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Sol Ring | Early acceleration or mana for repeated activations. Compare the resulting available mana with an actual payoff; it is not automatically the best removal target. |
| Arcane Signet | Colored acceleration. Artifact restrictions or removal can affect both its mana quantity and color access. |
| Fellwar Stone | Acceleration whose available colors depend on opponents' lands. |
| Mind Stone | Colorless acceleration that can later be exchanged for a card. This is not The Mind Stone. |
| Thought Vessel | Colorless acceleration plus hand-size relief. It does not itself draw cards. |
| Wayfarer's Bauble | Converts an artifact and activation mana into a tapped basic land, moving development out of the artifact zone. |
| Nature's Lore | Finds a Forest card, not necessarily a basic land. Whether it enters untapped also depends on that land's own text. |
| Three Visits | The same Forest-search role; it can supply colors beyond green through suitable land types. |
| Farseek | Finds one of its specified land types tapped, potentially fixing several colors through a nonbasic. |
| Rampant Growth | Basic-land acceleration into a later turn. The searched land enters tapped. |
| Cultivate | One basic onto the battlefield tapped and another into hand: acceleration plus support for a later land drop. |
| Kodama's Reach | The same land-development pattern as Cultivate, with a different card name and subtype. |
| Sprouting Vines | An instant that finds a basic land to hand, with storm supplying additional searches for spells cast before it that turn. It can use opponents' spell activity; copies are not extra spells cast for storm. |
| Tithe | Finds a Plains card to hand and can find another if an opponent controls more lands. These need not be basic Plains; this is not Smothering Tithe or direct battlefield ramp. |
| Land Tax | A conditional upkeep search for up to three basics to hand. An opponent must control more lands both for the trigger and when it resolves. |
| Weathered Wayfarer | A repeatable paid tap ability finding any land to hand, usable only while an opponent controls more lands. Utility-land access and summoning sickness matter. |
| Birds of Paradise | Cheap creature-based fixing and acceleration, with creature-removal and summoning-sickness dependencies. |
| Llanowar Elves | Cheap green acceleration through a creature's tap ability. It can also matter as an Elf or expendable body. |
| Sakura-Tribe Elder | Sacrifices itself to put a basic land onto the battlefield tapped, converting a creature into land development. |
| Solemn Simulacrum | Basic-land acceleration on entry and a card on death; recurring the body can repeat those roles. Exile is not death. |
| Wild Growth | Adds green mana when its enchanted land is tapped for mana. Land untaps can reuse it; land removal can remove both investments. |
| Fertile Ground | A land Aura that adds a mana of any color when the enchanted land is tapped for mana. Its fixing differs from Wild Growth. |
| Coalition Relic | Fixes colors and can store a charge for a later precombat main phase. Distinguish stored burst mana from its ordinary tap production. |
| Seething Song | Converts three mana into five red mana for a temporary burst. It advances a particular sequence rather than establishing a permanent mana source. |

The [Commander Masters release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-masters-release-notes) explain Land Tax's conditional trigger and basic-land restriction. Do not assume that the same conditions or search restrictions apply to Tithe or Weathered Wayfarer.

## Selection and Draw

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Ponder | Top-of-library selection with an optional shuffle. It is not a repeatable draw engine by itself. |
| Preordain | Scry-based selection and a replacement card, not automatic net card advantage. |
| Brainstorm | Immediate card access followed by putting two cards back; shuffle effects can change its longer-term value. |
| Night's Whisper | Trades mana and life for additional cards without establishing a permanent engine. |
| Sign in Blood | A targeted draw-and-life-loss spell; it can be directed at someone other than its caster. |
| Read the Bones | Selection followed by draw and life loss. The caster is replenishing options rather than adding a board engine. |
| Harmonize | Straightforward one-shot replenishment. |
| Rishkar's Expertise | A large creature can fuel a large draw and a follow-up spell. Check the board on resolution, not just when it was cast. |
| Return of the Wildspeaker | Either non-Human-based draw or a combat boost. Do not assume the chosen mode before it is announced. |
| Beast Whisperer | Creature spells become cards. Casting and entering the battlefield are different events. |
| Guardian Project | Conditional draw from nontoken creature entries, with name checks. Token production alone does not feed it. |
| Welcoming Vampire | Small-creature entries can draw once each turn, including opponents' turns when suitable entries occur. |
| Tocasia's Welcome | Low-mana-value creature entries can draw once each turn. Check mana value rather than power. |
| Brainstone | A colorless artifact offering draw-three, put-two-back selection through a paid tap-and-sacrifice activation. It costs more total mana than Brainstorm but can fit different colors, timing plans, and artifact synergies. |
| Faithless Looting | Draws and discards, with flashback allowing later reuse. It can improve card quality and stock a graveyard without creating net cards in hand by itself. |
| Fact or Fiction | An opponent divides revealed cards into piles, and the caster chooses one for hand while the other enters the graveyard. Both destinations can support the plan. |
| Frantic Search | Draws, discards, and untaps lands. The mana actually recovered depends on the lands; selection and graveyard setup are distinct from net card gain. |
| Deadly Dispute | Sacrifices an artifact or creature as a cost for cards and a Treasure. An expendable input can make the exchange more useful than raw draw alone. |
| Village Rites | A cheap sacrifice-to-draw spell requiring a creature. It cannot use an ordinary noncreature artifact in place of that creature. |
| Idol of Oblivion | A tap-to-draw artifact enabled by having created a token that turn. It needs an actual token event and does not supply repeatable tokens itself. |
| Bident of Thassa | Creature combat damage to players becomes cards. Multiple connections can matter; it also has a separate ability affecting opposing attacks. |

## Spot Interaction

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Swords to Plowshares | Cheap creature exile with compensating life gain; destruction protection is not enough. |
| Path to Exile | Cheap creature exile that may give the controller a tapped basic land. |
| Generous Gift | Broad permanent destruction with a replacement creature. |
| Beast Within | Broad permanent destruction with a replacement creature. Its ability to hit lands can matter. |
| Chaos Warp | Shuffles a permanent away, then may replace it with a permanent from the library. It is not simple destruction. |
| Counterspell | Unconditional spell countering for two blue mana, subject to countering restrictions. |
| Arcane Denial | A counterspell with delayed card compensation for both players. |
| Swan Song | Efficient but limited to enchantment, instant, or sorcery spells; it supplies a replacement creature. |
| Negate | Noncreature-spell interaction, not an answer to a creature spell or an already-resolving ability. |
| Anguished Unmaking | Instant-speed exile of a nonland permanent at a life cost. |
| Assassin's Trophy | Destroys an opposing permanent and may compensate its controller with a basic land. |
| Abrade | Chooses between creature damage and artifact destruction. Its flexibility does not include enchantments or every creature size. |
| Return to Nature | Chooses artifact destruction, enchantment destruction, or exile of a card from a graveyard. One mode is not all three at once. |
| Nature's Claim | Cheap artifact or enchantment destruction with compensating life gain. |
| Feed the Swarm | Black sorcery-speed destruction of an opposing creature or enchantment, paid for in life as well as mana. |
| Despark | Exiles a permanent with mana value four or greater. It misses many small but important engine pieces. |
| Vindicate | Sorcery-speed destruction of any permanent, including a land. Its breadth trades against the need to act in one's main phase. |
| Resculpt | Exiles an artifact or creature and replaces it with a creature token. Compare its target range and replacement body with other blue answers. |
| Pongify | Cheap creature destruction with a replacement body. It does not solve indestructible in the same way as exile. |
| Reality Shift | Exiles a creature and has its controller manifest the top card of their library. The replacement may become a later resource. |
| Dovin's Veto | An uncounterable counterspell for noncreature spells. It does not counter an activated or triggered ability. |
| An Offer You Can't Refuse | Cheap noncreature-spell countering that gives the spell's controller two Treasures, potentially helping their next attempt. |

## Board Resets

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Blasphemous Act | A crowded battlefield can make its damage-based creature reset inexpensive. Toughness and damage prevention matter. |
| Wrath of God | Creature destruction that prevents regeneration; indestructible and exile are different questions. |
| Damnation | The corresponding black creature-destruction reset. |
| Austere Command | A choice of modes can remove selected categories while leaving the caster's preferred resources. |
| Vandalblast | Can answer one opposing artifact or, when overloaded, all opposing artifacts. |
| Toxic Deluge | Uses a life payment to give creatures negative power and toughness. Indestructible does not stop death from zero or less toughness. |
| Supreme Verdict | An uncounterable creature-destruction reset. That protection from counters does not make it exile or stop every form of interaction. |
| Vanquish the Horde | A crowded battlefield reduces the generic cost of this creature-destruction reset. |
| Chain Reaction | Creature-count-based damage to every creature. Survivors depend on the resulting damage, toughness, and protection. |
| Nevinyrral's Disk | A colorless reset for artifacts, creatures, and enchantments, with an enters-tapped delay and a paid tap activation. Its visible setup changes the response window. |

## Protection

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Heroic Intervention | Hexproof and indestructible can protect a board, but not from every sacrifice, exile, or toughness-reduction effect. |
| Boros Charm | One mode protects permanents with indestructible; other modes serve damage or combat purposes. |
| Tamiyo's Safekeeping | Cheap protection for a single permanent with incidental life gain. |
| Lightning Greaves | Haste and shroud after attachment. Equip timing and the inability to target one's own shrouded creature matter. |
| Swiftfoot Boots | Haste and hexproof after attachment; unlike shroud, hexproof still permits its controller's targeting. |
| Flawless Maneuver | Indestructible for the caster's creatures, with an alternative free cost when they control a commander. It does not protect all permanent types. |
| Deflecting Swat | Changes targets of a spell or ability, potentially for no mana while controlling a commander. It is not a universal answer to untargeted effects. |
| Malakir Rebirth | Gives a creature a one-use death-return trigger at a life cost, with a land on the reverse face. Exile and repeated deaths are different cases. |
| Tyvar's Stand | Protects a creature with hexproof and indestructible while also allowing a paid power-and-toughness boost. Its roles include combat as well as survival. |

Protection is a possibility, not an assumed card in hand. Assess known information and usable mana. Neither an equipment on the battlefield nor a protection spell somewhere in a list guarantees a protected line.

## Graveyards and Recursion

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Soul-Guide Lantern | A visible graveyard-management tool with distinct entry and sacrifice abilities. |
| Scavenging Ooze | Repeated targeted graveyard exile constrained by available green mana. |
| Eternal Witness | Returns a card to hand; repeated entry or recursion can turn that into a continuing engine. |
| Sun Titan | Attack and entry triggers retrieve low-mana-value permanents, including appropriate lands. |
| Reanimate | Converts a creature card in any graveyard into a battlefield threat at a life cost. |
| Animate Dead | Recursion linked to an Aura, creating additional interaction points and dependencies. Check exact wording for a loop. |
| Victimize | Trades a creature on the battlefield for two targeted creature cards returned tapped from the caster's graveyard. The sacrifice happens during resolution, unlike an additional casting cost. |
| Persist | Returns a nonlegendary creature from the caster's graveyard with a -1/-1 counter. Its eligibility differs from unrestricted reanimation. |
| Sevinne's Reclamation | Returns a low-mana-value permanent, with flashback and an additional copy when cast from a graveyard. Lands can be useful targets. |
| Regrowth | Returns any card from the caster's graveyard to hand. It retrieves an option but does not cast it for free. |
| Tormod's Crypt | A zero-mana artifact that sacrifices to exile a targeted player's graveyard. It is visible, one-use interaction without another source of recursion. |
| Endurance | A flash creature whose entry can put a player's graveyard on the bottom of their library. Its evoke option uses a green card from hand, not no resources at all. |

## Combat Conversion

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Overwhelming Stampede | An established creature board can become a much shorter clock through a shared boost and trample. |
| Akroma's Will | Can turn a board into an evasive, protected attack. Whether both modes are available depends on controlling a commander. |
| Craterhoof Behemoth | Creature density becomes an immediate team-wide boost and trample. A sparse board is a different threat from a full one. |
| Beastmaster Ascension | Attack triggers build toward a team-wide boost. Several attackers can reach the threshold in one combat; damage need not connect. |
| Shared Animosity | Shared creature types among attackers become additional attacking power. It changes the damage clock without boosting toughness. |
| Reconnaissance | Lets its controller remove their own attacking creatures from combat and untap them. Exact combat-step timing matters; it does not by itself grant evasion. |
| Coat of Arms | Shared creature types increase power and toughness across the battlefield. It can strengthen opponents' creatures as well as its controller's. |

## Utility Lands

This section includes fixing lands as well as lands with additional utility. Evaluate colored sources, enters-tapped costs, activation costs, and the spell requirements of the whole deck. A useful land is not automatically a safe replacement for a colored source.

| Card | What to recognize across the table |
| --- | --- |
| Command Tower | Color fixing tied to commander color identity, not extra mana production. |
| Exotic Orchard | Color access depends on opponents' lands. |
| Reliquary Tower | Removes maximum hand size but produces only colorless mana and does not draw cards. |
| Path of Ancestry | Enters tapped and fixes commander-identity colors. Spending its mana on a creature sharing a type with the commander produces a scry; it is not a scry for every spell. |
| Cavern of Souls | Its restricted colored mana helps cast creatures of a chosen type and makes those spells uncounterable. Its unrestricted colorless mana does not carry that protection. |
| City of Brass | Produces any color but triggers damage whenever it becomes tapped, including when another effect taps it. |
| Mana Confluence | Produces any color by paying life as an activation cost. Paying life and taking City of Brass's triggered damage are not interchangeable mechanics. |
| Cascading Cataracts | An indestructible land that can filter a five-mana payment into five mana in chosen colors. This is fixing, not a five-mana profit. |
| The World Tree | Enters tapped, then gives lands broad fixing at six lands; a separate expensive sacrifice ability finds Gods. These are distinct roles and requirements. |
| Academy Ruins | A paid tap ability puts an artifact from the graveyard on top of its controller's library. It does not directly draw or deploy that card. |
| Mystic Sanctuary | An Island that can return an instant or sorcery to the library top if it enters untapped. Its normal untapped entry requires three other Islands. |
| Gemstone Caverns | Can begin on the battlefield with a luck counter from an opening hand when its player is not starting, at the cost of exiling another card from hand. Otherwise it is normally a colorless source. |
| Strip Mine | Trades itself for destruction of a targeted land. It can answer a utility land or attack mana access, with different table expectations for repeated use. |
| Bojuka Bog | Enters tapped and exiles a targeted player's graveyard through an entry trigger. Playing the land is not casting a spell. |
| Rogue's Passage | A land-based mana sink that makes a creature unblockable for the turn, potentially converting size or combat triggers into a finish. |

[Path of Ancestry's release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-masters-release-notes) explain the shared-type condition and the mana's link to the spell. A land's fixing, restrictions, and utility should be explained separately.

## Recognizing Alternatives

Start with the needed function and search the player's supplied pool for ways to perform it. Compare total mana, timing, colors, card types, actual card gain, replenishment, and support already present. Do not recommend a famous card automatically or dismiss an unfamiliar one as a worse copy. Equally, do not pretend that synergy eliminates a real cost or that a cheaper purchase price proves better fit.

**Brainstone and Brainstorm:** Both can provide draw-three, put-two-back selection. Brainstone costs one mana to deploy and two plus tapping and sacrificing it to activate, compared with Brainstorm's one blue mana. Its colorless identity, ability to spread the payment across turns, and artifact-related support may make it useful in a different pool. Recursion can allow reuse, but requires an actual available mechanism and its costs. The activation is not another spell cast. These are contextual differences, not a claim that Brainstone is always better or always worse.

**Land access is not interchangeable:** Tithe finds Plains cards, Land Tax finds basics conditionally during upkeep, and Weathered Wayfarer can repeatedly find any land while its activation condition holds. Sprouting Vines instead scales with prior spell casts and searches for basics. Which supports the deck depends on the actual mana base, timing, and engine, not their sharing a "land search" label.

**The body or permanent type may matter:** An available Sakura-Tribe Elder, Solemn Simulacrum, land Aura, or mana rock may perform development differently under sacrifice, recursion, untap, or restriction effects. Compare the supplied options and what each leaves behind; do not assume an unseen supporting card.

These named comparisons teach evaluation. They do not make either card owned or authorize an outside recommendation. If the pool lacks an adequate alternative, identify the missing function honestly and ask about a targeted outside search only when useful.

## Evidence and Use

Official preconstructed lists provide concrete examples of these kinds of tools: [Warhammer 40,000 Commander lists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/warhammer-40000-commander-decklists) include Sol Ring, Mind Stone, Thought Vessel, and Skullclamp; [Doctor Who Commander lists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/magic-the-gathering-doctor-who-commander-decklists) include Ponder, Preordain, Cultivate, and Beast Within; [Final Fantasy Commander lists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/final-fantasy-commander-decklists) include Reanimate, Sun Titan, and Walking Ballista. These examples are not a frequency study or evidence that every listed card is universally appropriate.
