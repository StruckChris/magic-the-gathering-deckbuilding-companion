# Creature Aggro and Tokens

## Recognize the Plan

The pool contains creatures or token makers that can establish pressure, plus ways to keep attacks relevant. White weenie is one historical expression of efficient creature aggro, not the only color or implementation.

## Shell Engine and Finish

The shell develops threats early and protects their opportunity to matter. The engine might be recurring token production with related draw, counters that grow inexpensive bodies, attack-trigger rewards, or a steady stream of individually useful creatures.

Finishers can enlarge an existing army, create evasion, add attacks, or supply a larger threat when the smaller bodies stop connecting. Identify those functions in supplied cards rather than prescribing a particular anthem or token package.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

More creatures are not always the best next play. Compare the extra pressure with preserving recovery after a sweep. A token count without a way to attack profitably can become a stalled board. An anthem without enough bodies can be an expensive do-nothing draw.

In multiplayer, plan beyond eliminating the first opponent. Test whether the same engine can keep producing material and whether attackers leave enough defense.

**Coaching question:** Which supplied cards make an early creature still useful several turns later?

## Historical Reference

**Primary: Reid Duke's White-Black Aggro, Standard, 2014 teaching list.** This is a published construction example, not an attributed tournament finish. The selected package is 4 Soldier of the Pantheon, 4 Bloodsoaked Champion, 4 Chief of the Edge, and 2 Mogis's Marauder. [Duke's aggro primer and full list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/aggro-decks-2014-09-29).

The shell deploys inexpensive pressure; mutually useful bodies, enhancement, and ways past blockers convert development into damage. This 2014 teaching example makes the older efficient-creature method accessible without requiring the same colors or cards.

## Sustainable Advantage

Across games, interchangeable early plays make the plan less dependent on one opening creature. For Commander, look for owned token production, attack rewards, or replenishment that lets ordinary early bodies remain useful later. Existing attackers can keep contributing when casting is restricted, but they still need legal, profitable attacks and a way to replace losses. Efficient deployment alone does not solve multiplayer's larger damage requirement.

## Evolution and Overlap

[Zoo](21-zoo.md) emphasizes efficient multicolor pressure; [typal](18-typal.md) rewards and [aristocrats](08-aristocrats.md) can give the same bodies different jobs. Borrowing those engines can improve continuity without abandoning an aggressive shell. Classify the method actually supported by the pool, not just the presence of small creatures.
