# Enchantress

## Recognize the Plan

The pool rewards playing enchantments, often by replenishing cards or accumulating other resources. Enchantment density alone does not prove that those rewards can be triggered usefully and repeatedly.

## Shell Engine and Finish

The shell organizes development around enchantments and their supporting payoffs. Decide whether its intended role is aggressive enhancement, control, or a longer resource engine. The engine links useful enchantment actions to draw, recursion, tokens, or other supplied benefits.

Finishers could scale an established board, create a protected threat, or convert accumulated resources through a particular interaction. A protected pile of enchantments still needs a way to end the game.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

Cast triggers and entry triggers reward different actions. Returning an enchantment directly to play may not trigger the card the player expects. Auras also need legal objects to enchant, and moving them around is not necessarily casting them again.

Ask whether the enchantments do necessary work without the draw payoff. Too many narrow enablers can make the deck depend on drawing one specific support card. Check how the supplied recovery works after removal rather than assuming enchantments are untouchable.

**Coaching question:** If the draw payoff is absent, which of these enchantments would you still be glad to deploy?

[Author's list and discussion](https://articles.starcitygames.com/articles/the-wrong-goodbye-survival-saito-and-me/).

Draw sustains a defensive position while token generation supplies the finish.

## Sustainable Advantage

Across games, several compatible draw enablers and enchantments that independently perform needed jobs can reduce fragile hands. In Commander, distinguish maintaining protection from advancing a clock behind it. An established enchantment engine may avoid reliance on artifact mana, but can still depend on casting, fresh cards, mana, or upkeep payments. Name which parts continue under a particular restriction.

## Evolution and Overlap

Enchantress connects with [Voltron](02-voltron.md) through enhancements and [prison](07-prison-and-stax.md) through maintained defenses. Its engine can shift between attacks, tokens, and resource conversion while preserving enchantment-driven replenishment. Not every enchantment-heavy pool contains that replenishment; recognize the actual mechanism before applying the label.
