# Combo Shells

## Recognize the Plan

The supplied cards contain an interaction with a defined, meaningful output. Choose whether the surrounding shell primarily assembles it, protects it through control, or advances another plan with the combo as an alternative finish.

## Shell Engine and Finish

The shell supplies access, resources, and time. The engine is the particular interaction network. The finisher is how that network ends the game, not merely the fact that it produces many resources.

Build a precise line from owned cards: starting zones, costs, sequence, output, and outlet. A second combination can improve resilience when it shares useful components without making the whole deck depend on the same vulnerable permanent.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

Two-card interactions are not automatically better for this player's experience than three- or four-card engines. Independently useful pieces can matter more than minimum piece count. Conversely, a collection of partial combos can dilute the ability to complete any one of them.

Check colored mana, timing, summoning sickness, required targets, once-per-turn restrictions, and whether resources actually renew. Do not promise infinity from a sequence that consumes a finite hand or library without replacement.

**Coaching question:** If the first attempt is stopped, what can the remaining cards do next?

## Historical Reference

**Primary: Kai Budde's historical Extended High Tide, reproduced in the 2008 retrospective.** Selected package: 4 High Tide, 4 Time Spiral, 3 Turnabout, and 3 Stroke of Genius. [Published list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/top-10-extended-decks-all-time-2008-02-07).

Mana enhancement, untapping, and replenishment support a spell chain with an outlet. This is a resource-conversion example, not a fixed two-card combo.

## Sustainable Advantage

Use [two-, three-, and four-card combo accounting](../primers/05-combo-structure.md) for a specific supplied line. Include the commander, setup, and any extra winning outlet. Across games, consider useful partial packages, redundant functions, and another attempt after disruption. Extra combinations do not provide independent resilience if they all require the same unavailable resource. A casting restriction may stop a spell chain while a legal established ability loop behaves differently; verify both.

## Evolution and Overlap

Combo is often an ending inside another shell: [typal](18-typal.md), [artifacts](14-artifact-synergy.md), or [aristocrats](08-aristocrats.md) may develop normally before assembling it. A control shell can buy time for several attempts. Choose the engine that fits the player's idea and supplied pool, not a universal smallest-combo standard.
