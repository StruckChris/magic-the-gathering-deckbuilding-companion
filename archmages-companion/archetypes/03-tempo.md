# Tempo and Aggro Control

## Recognize the Plan

Efficient threats are paired with disruption that keeps opponents from using their resources effectively at the important moment. The deck uses the gained window rather than trying to answer everything permanently.

## Shell Engine and Finish

The shell establishes a threat and preserves favorable exchanges through timing, bounce, counters, tapping, or other supplied interference. The engine can be repeated evasive attacks with card access, useful entry effects, or flexible actions that develop the board while leaving choices open.

Finishers shorten the remaining clock or secure the last attack. Merely returning a permanent to hand is not progress if the deck cannot exploit the time gained.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

Do not automatically exchange a flexible or free response for a generically stronger card that requires unavailable mana. Count the actual turn's options. Conversely, temporary disruption that repeatedly consumes cards without advancing a threat can run out of resources.

Multiplayer adds opponents who may benefit from someone else's lost tempo. Ask whether the gained window helps this deck enough, and retain tools for ongoing development.

**Coaching question:** What can your board accomplish before the opponent can use that resource again?

## Historical Reference

**Primary: Alan Comer's Miracle Grow, Extended, ninth at Grand Prix Las Vegas 2001.** Selected package: 4 Quirion Dryad, 4 Winter Orb, 4 Daze, and 4 Gush. [Published list in the 2008 Extended retrospective](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/top-10-extended-decks-all-time-2008-02-07).

An inexpensive growing threat operates alongside cheap interaction and mana denial. Pressure makes the restricted window consequential.

## Sustainable Advantage

A Commander translation needs multiple useful threat-and-interaction pairings rather than one perfect sequence. Look for supplied attacks that replenish resources, flexible answers that fit the curve, and productive uses of held mana. Established threats can operate under some mana restrictions, but new deployment and protection may still consume that mana. Test who actually benefits from the time gained when three opponents are developing.

## Evolution and Overlap

Tempo overlaps with [prison](07-prison-and-stax.md) when restrictions preserve an early lead, and with [Draw-Go](05-draw-go.md) when instant-speed choices matter. It usually needs its clock established earlier than a pure attrition plan. Fish and Vial/Still are related historical discussions that require their own sourced list when requested, not interchangeable names for every threat-plus-counterspell deck.
