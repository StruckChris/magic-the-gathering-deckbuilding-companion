# Artifact Synergy

## Recognize the Plan

Artifacts share a card type, not automatically a strategy. The pool needs a functional connection: resource production, sacrifice, copying, combat scaling, cost reduction, or another supplied reward.

## Shell Engine and Finish

Choose whether the deck is artifact aggro, combo, control, or a flexible value deck. Its engine might reuse expendable artifacts, copy useful permanents, convert tokens into resources, or animate accumulated material for attacks.

Finishers use the output: an artifact army, a scalable effect, or a verified resource loop with an outlet. Count whether the same artifacts are needed simultaneously for mana, sacrifice, and combat; competing uses are not free overlap.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

Check colored casting costs and activation restrictions despite a largely colorless appearance. An artifact becoming a creature changes which actions are possible, including tap abilities and attachment interactions. Verify the exact copy or animation effect.

Shared vulnerability can matter when many roles rely on artifacts, but do not invent an opposing artifact sweeper every game. Look for supplied recovery and independent lines that preserve the chosen identity.

**Coaching question:** What resource does this artifact package convert, and how does that conversion continue after the first use?

## Sustainable Advantage

Identify what supplied artifacts do besides occupy the type line. Across games, inexpensive pieces that support several conversions can make partial engines useful, while draw or reuse supplies another round of actions. Established abilities may function when spell casting is restricted, but artifact shutdown can disable several roles together. Count that shared exposure rather than claiming several artifact mana sources are independent resilience.

## Evolution and Overlap

Artifacts can support [aggro](01-creature-aggro.md), [prison](07-prison-and-stax.md), or [combo](12-combo.md); Affinity is one aggressive branch. A casual copy-and-token engine need not become a sacrifice deck to learn from its resource efficiency. Preserve the player's preferred finish while identifying which interactions make ordinary artifacts contribute repeatedly.
