# Voltron

## Recognize the Plan

The pool can concentrate enhancements on a commander or another suitable creature. Voltron describes making a small number of attackers unusually threatening; it is not restricted to Equipment or to commander damage.

## Shell Engine and Finish

The shell combines a credible attacker, enhancements, ways to connect, and enough protection or recovery to retain the investment. The engine might draw from casting enhancements, reuse Equipment between creatures, or retrieve pieces after removal.

A finisher is the particular combination that creates a short, legal damage clock. Check the actual damage calculation and whether the claimed victory depends on commander combat damage rather than ordinary life loss.

## Decisions and Failure Modes

Count casting and attachment costs, not just the printed price of an enhancement. Several improvements on one creature can lose substantial resources to one answer. Reusable Equipment still needs another body and may need more mana to attach again.

Backup attackers and recast plans matter, but do not automatically remove the player's favored commander-centered identity. Ask how opponents are expected to interact and what recovery the supplied pool supports.

**Coaching question:** If the enhanced attacker disappears, what part of the investment remains useful next turn?

## Historical Reference

**Primary: Reid Duke's Hexproof, Modern, 2013 World Championship.** The official deck-tech description supplies the list. Selected package: 4 Slippery Bogle, 4 Gladecover Scout, 4 Kor Spiritdancer, 4 Ethereal Armor, and 4 Daybreak Coronet. [Official deck tech and published list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-xEoMEzkN4).

This is a competitive concentrated-enhancement analogue, not a commander-damage list. Protected attackers carry stacked improvements while supporting draw helps replenish the investment.

## Sustainable Advantage

Redundant suitable bodies and improvements let different draws build a similar attacking threat. Translate that into available backup carriers, affordable attachment or casting costs, and card flow. An established attacker may keep working through a spell limit, but removal, sacrifice requirements, blocked attacks, and rebuilding costs remain relevant. Do not count protection from one kind of interaction as protection from every answer.

## Evolution and Overlap

Voltron borrows from [creature aggro](01-creature-aggro.md), [Enchantress](15-enchantress.md), and [artifact synergy](14-artifact-synergy.md). Aura-driven replenishment and reusable Equipment are different engines inside a concentrated-combat plan. Changing the engine changes recovery and resource demands, even when the desired finish remains one threatening attacker.
