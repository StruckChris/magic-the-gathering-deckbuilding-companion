# Sources and Verification

Historical sources and EDHREC pages inspected on 1 October 2026; the Scryfall access limitation is recorded below. This is a compact reference directory, not a frozen authority for future rules or metagames. Use current evidence for current questions.

The teaching sequence, working definitions, and collection-only policy are teaching conventions, not claims to have invented shell, engine, or finisher. The author's account of learning this vocabulary on 2000s-era forums and playing Legacy Vial/Still is author-provided public background, recorded in the [identity instructions](BOT_INSTRUCTIONS.md#identity). It is not an independently researched history of the terms' first use.

The primers are original instructional synthesis, not quotations or endorsements from the linked authors. Specific historical and competitive claims have local citations in the relevant guides and archetype primers.

## Rules

- [Magic rules hub](https://magic.wizards.com/en/rules): entry point for current basic and Comprehensive Rules, including timing, copying, and construction exceptions.
- [Commander format](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander): introductory rules. Specific exceptions need detailed rules and card text.
- [Banned and Restricted](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list): official format lists. Also verify eligibility; not every ineligible card is banned.
- [Commander Legends release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06): Laboratory Drudge's end-step timing. An old release note does not supersede later Oracle or rules updates.
- [Guild to Order Part 1, September 10, 2018](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/guild-order-part-1-2018-09-10): official jump-start explanation for the bounded example.

The official rules and Commander pages link to the publisher's card database. Use current text rather than relying only on memory or a printed image.

The bounded alternative-deployment examples also use [Narset's official design explanation](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/prose-and-khans-part-2-2014-09-22) and the [Kaldheim release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2021/downloads/KHM_Release_Notes/EN_MTGKHM_ReleaseNotes_20210113.pdf) for The Prismatic Bridge. They explain different mechanisms, not interchangeable casting permissions.

## Construction and History

- [Level One full course, October 5, 2015](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/level-one-full-course-2015-10-05): Reid Duke's fundamentals directory, not a list of current card prescriptions.
- [Aggro Decks, September 29, 2014](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/aggro-decks-2014-09-29): historical 60-card teaching example and aggressive-development context.
- [Control Decks, October 6, 2014](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/control-decks-2014-10-06): historical control teaching example.
- [Building The Aristocrats, February 20, 2013](https://articles.starcitygames.com/articles/building-the-aristocrats/): Sam Black's firsthand construction account. Its primary-source status comes from the deck designer's authorship, not the retailer. Some migrated card references are incomplete; do not reconstruct missing text by guesswork.
- [Top 10 Extended Decks of All Time, February 7, 2008](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/top-10-extended-decks-all-time-2008-02-07): historical lists including High Tide. Use the resource-conversion example, not the ranking as evidence of Commander suitability.

The following mana and mulligan references were checked on 4 October 2026. Their application to the companion's guided land-count conversation is explained in [Commander fundamentals](guides/commander-fundamentals.md#recommend-a-starting-land-count):

- [Building a Mana Base, November 24, 2014](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/building-mana-base-2014-11-24): Reid Duke on colored requirements and iterative mana-base construction. Its 60-card source-count guidelines are not Commander targets.
- [Mulligans Part III: Constructed, June 29, 2015](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/mulligans-part-iii-constructed-2015-06-29): Duke on knowing the deck, recognizing its opening needs, and weighing keep decisions. Use the strategic reasoning, not obsolete procedures or assumed tournament pace.
- [Famous Red Decks in Magic History, July 22, 2004](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/famous-red-decks-magic-history-2004-07-22-0): Alex Shvartsman's publisher-hosted retrospective reproduces Paul Sligh's 1996 list as 61 cards with 23 lands and explains the curve-oriented lineage.
- [Boss Sligh in M15, July 17, 2014](https://articles.starcitygames.com/articles/boss-sligh-in-m15/): Tom Ross's firsthand account presents his 17-land pre-M15 list and a proposed 18-land revision. The lists and headings remain readable despite some missing inline card-name references; do not reconstruct missing names. Together with the earlier Sligh example, this illustrates different mana needs within a lineage, not a format-wide time-series study.
